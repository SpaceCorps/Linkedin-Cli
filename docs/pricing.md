## 2. Apify Platform Pricing

LinkedIn CLI queries Apify to execute profile and post scraping actors. Running these actors consumes Apify compute units (CUs) and actor usage credits.

- **Apify Free Tier:** Apify provides a recurring monthly free credit allowance that allows developers to run hundreds of profile lookups and post extractions each month at no cost.
- **Paid Tiers:** For high-volume enterprise scraping or production pipelines, refer to the official [Apify Pricing Page](https://apify.com/pricing).

SpaceCorps is not affiliated with Apify and does not collect any fees or commissions for actor runs.
